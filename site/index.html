Proof layer / Hygge Coffee Shop

A generic offer vs. a data-driven offer.

A. Control — no data

Generic offer

“Hi, would you like to give away 5 free coffees on weekdays to bring students to your café?”

Could be sent to any café; uses no review, price or busyness data.

B. Personalised for Hygge

Data-driven offer

“Let’s put your calm back garden at the centre of the Ders Arası Drop pilot: 5 free filter coffees, weekdays 14:00–16:00 as a starting point. The item and the time are yours to decide; this is a small pilot, not a paid partnership.”

Written from Hygge’s own reviews and busyness signals; claims no result or partnership.
